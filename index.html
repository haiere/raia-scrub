FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before you scrub your first file.

Are my files uploaded to a server?
No. Raia Scrub runs entirely inside your browser. Files are read locally with the File API and never leave your device. You can even disconnect from the internet after the page loads and keep scrubbing.
What metadata does Raia Scrub remove?
For images it removes EXIF data such as GPS coordinates, camera make and model, capture timestamps, software, artist, copyright, focal length, camera serial numbers and embedded thumbnails. For PDFs it clears the title, author, subject, keywords, creator, producer and the XMP metadata stream.
Which file formats are supported?
JPG, JPEG, PNG and PDF. You can queue up to 8 files at a time, with a maximum size of 50 MB per file.
Does scrubbing reduce image quality?
JPEG files are cleaned losslessly by rewriting the EXIF segment, so the pixels stay untouched. PNG files are re-encoded through a canvas at maximum quality, which removes metadata while preserving visual fidelity.
Is Raia Scrub free to use?
Yes. Raia Scrub is completely free and open source. There is no account, no sign-up, no watermark and no installation.
Can I use Raia Scrub offline?
Yes. After the first load, the page and its libraries are cached by your browser, so you can keep scrubbing files without an internet connection.
